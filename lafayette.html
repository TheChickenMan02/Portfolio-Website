Since 2020, online fashion has grown explosively, but that growth has mostly benefited a handful of giant retailers. Small and independent clothing brands get crowded out, leaving consumers with fewer truly unique or ethically made options.

VÔTY started as an open platform designed to fix that imbalance. For brand owners, it offered a simple way to display their full catalog to a wider audience. For shoppers, it offered a direct line to emerging fashion trends instead of algorithm driven feeds dominated by the same large players.

The model was built around real time analytics and a growing community of independent designers, with an onboarding process covering background checks, catalog submission, and compliance with shipping and ethical standards. Everyone in the loop benefits: influencers, brands, and customers all win through a shared fashion network and automated systems that keep the platform ahead of emerging trends.

The long term vision from day one was ambitious: become the largest U.S. based clothing manufacturer, with on demand production built directly into the platform.

Finalist.
