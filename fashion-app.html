The Fashion App
Founder & CEO · VÔTY · 2025

Online fashion discovery is broken. A shopper finds something on TikTok or Instagram, then has to bounce between comment sections, Discord servers, and a dozen resale apps just to track it down, and most people give up before they ever check out.

VÔTY closes that loop in one app. The home feed works like the social platforms people already use, except every post is instantly shoppable or remixable directly from the feed. The Explore page turns inspiration into curated lookbooks, categories, and brand drops for deeper discovery. Every profile doubles as a personal hub, combining a virtual closet with a running history of every listing, outfit, and order. The Earn section turns creativity into income, rewarding users for posting, styling, or selling, so influence becomes a revenue stream rather than just engagement.

The business model runs on three revenue streams: an 8 percent commission on cash outs, a 5 percent buyer protection fee on purchases, and paid placements and sponsored content across VÔTY media. The go to market strategy starts campus by campus, using engagement data from each school to expand city by city, turning pop up fashion events into experiences people want to repeat.

VÔTY is targeting the Gen Z and millennial fashion market, a reachable $4 billion segment, differentiating from single purpose platforms like Depop for resale, StockX for trust, and Pinterest for inspiration by bringing discovery, shopping, and earning together into one app.
