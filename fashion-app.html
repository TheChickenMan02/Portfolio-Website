Online fashion discovery is broken. A shopper finds something on TikTok or Instagram, then has to bounce between comment sections, Discord servers, and a dozen resale apps just to track it down — and most people give up before they ever check out.

VÔTY closes that loop in one app. The home feed works like the social platforms people already use, except every post is instantly shoppable or remixable. The Explore page turns inspiration into curated lookbooks and drops. Every profile doubles as a virtual closet — tracking listings, orders, and wishlists in one place. And the Earn section turns influence into income: post, style, or sell, and get paid for it.

The business model is three revenue streams: an 8% commission on sales, a 5% buyer protection fee, and paid placements/sponsored content — targeting a reachable $4B segment of the Gen Z and millennial fashion market.
