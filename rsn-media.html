RSN Media was a creative media agency built to help gyms and fitness studios across Acadiana grow without the overhead of an expensive marketing team or ad spend they could not justify. Clients included CrossFit Whiteboard and Ignite Fitness LA in Lafayette, Louisiana. The pitch was simple: lower a gym's cost of marketing so that money could go back into equipment, staff, or the business itself, with a guarantee that if the campaign did not perform, the marketing was free. Over time the client base expanded beyond gyms to local service businesses as well.

Every engagement started with a detailed onboarding questionnaire covering goals, current marketing spend, pricing, competitors, and brand identity. From there, RSN Media built targeted Facebook ad funnels that captured lead information and routed it directly into GoHighLevel, an all in one CRM, automatically starting a sales and retargeting sequence for every new lead. Metricool was used to plan and schedule social content, track engagement, and benchmark performance against competitors, so every strategy was backed by real data rather than guesswork.

Within the first 72 hours of one client's Google Ads campaign going live, it started gaining traction, and by the end of a 14 day trial the account had tripled its return on ad spend. Over the following four months, that same client's website traffic nearly doubled. Another client saw a similar jump after new blog content, an updated website, and targeted keyword phrases were built out, with client volume and value nearly doubling within two months, enough that the client was able to lower their own ad budget.
