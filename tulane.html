Tulane Venture Challenge · 2026

At the Tulane Venture Challenge, VÔTY pitched its vision to become the mall of the future: one app to help people discover, shop, and earn from fashion together. Today's $1 trillion online fashion market is broken. Someone finds an item on TikTok or Instagram, then has to dig through comment sections, Discord servers, and a dozen resale apps just to find where to buy it, often giving up before they ever check out.

VÔTY closes that loop through a single home feed, an Explore page for discovery, a profile that doubles as a virtual closet, and an Earn section that pays users for posting, styling, or selling. The business model runs on three revenue streams: an 8 percent commission on cash outs, a 5 percent buyer protection fee, and paid placements and sponsored content.

VÔTY is targeting the Gen Z and millennial fashion market, a reachable $4 billion segment, competing against platforms that each solve one piece of the problem: Depop for resale, StockX for trust, Pinterest for inspiration, but none for the full loop. The team is raising $250,000 on a SAFE with a $2.5 million cap, with a goal of reaching 150,000 active users and $3 million in gross merchandise value within six months.
