Tulane Venture Challenge · 2026

The way people discover and shop for fashion changes constantly, but the platforms built to support that behavior have not kept up. In a one trillion dollar online fashion market, someone finds an item on TikTok or Instagram, then has to jump between comment sections, resale apps, and shopping sites just to track it down. Most people give up somewhere in that process.

VÔTY turns any moment into a shoppable experience. See an outfit, snap a photo, and instantly discover, personalize, and buy it, all inside one app. The platform is powered by VÔTY AI, an agent that scans and tags items for one tap checkout, personalizes every recommendation based on real behavior, and is powered by a community of creators and brands posting shoppable looks, strengthening the feedback loop with every interaction.

After pivoting from a streetwear marketplace into a full app, VÔTY reached forty thousand total users, twenty thousand dollars in brand revenue, an average of over one hundred thousand monthly social views, and built a waitlist of over one thousand people ahead of launch. The opportunity is enormous: a one trillion dollar global fashion commerce market, a two hundred seventy two billion dollar U.S. online fashion segment, and an immediately reachable four billion dollar slice made up of Gen Z and Millennial shoppers.

The business model runs on three revenue streams: an eight percent commission on cash outs plus a five percent buyer protection fee, in app placement ads, and sponsored content and brand partnerships through VÔTY Media. Go to market follows three steps: See It, sparking discovery through fashion creator events where people can experience VÔTY in real life; Snap It, encouraging brands, creators, and users to capture and share outfits directly on the platform; and Shop It, closing the loop by partnering with brands, boutiques, and resale shops for exclusive in app drops.

VÔTY is the only fashion platform bringing discovery, AI powered scanning, fast checkout, creator earnings, and built in resale together in one place, where every competitor, from Pinterest and Instagram to eBay, Depop, and StockX, only solves one piece of the puzzle. The team is raising two hundred fifty thousand dollars on a SAFE with a two point five million dollar cap, with half of the funds going directly to team and product, aiming to reach one hundred fifty thousand active users and three million dollars in gross merchandise value within six months.
