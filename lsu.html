J. Terrell Brown Venture Challenge · 2025

At the 2025 J. Terrell Brown Venture Challenge at LSU, VÔTY pitched its plan to fix a broken shopping cycle. Fashion discovery online is chaotic: someone finds an outfit on Instagram or TikTok, then has to jump between apps and sites just to buy it, with no simple way to resell it afterward. That friction wastes over 100 million tons of clothing every year, the equivalent of 16 Pyramids of Giza.

VÔTY's fix closes that loop in a single app: snap a photo of an outfit to buy it or a verified alternative instantly, then resell it back into the community once you are done wearing it. The plan presented at LSU centered on an official partnership with Posh, an event ticketing company, to run campus takeovers at LSU and Southern University, turning fashion pop up events into a direct pipeline of new users, brand partners, and local influencers.

The team requested $25,000 to finish the app and fund early growth: $11,000 toward app development, $8,000 toward hosting local fashion events, $3,000 toward outreach and content, and $1,900 toward filing trademarks.
