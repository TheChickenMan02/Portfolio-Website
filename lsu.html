You spend weeks perfecting a design, sink hundreds into inventory, launch, and hear nothing back. That is the reality for most small streetwear brands. Sixty seven percent of shoppers do not trust small clothing brands, and more than thirty percent of online buyers aged sixteen to thirty five report late deliveries, wrong items, or poor quality from Instagram based brands. With cart abandonment in fashion sitting near eighty eight percent, streetwear needed a space where trust came first.

VÔTY is a streetwear marketplace built to make discovering and shopping from trusted, up and coming brands simple. Small brands get the spotlight and credibility they are missing through content and media, while shoppers get a place to find unique pieces they can actually trust. Over eighty percent of small streetwear brands fail from a simple lack of visibility and trust, and VÔTY closes that gap by curating credible brands and turning culture into conversions.

The core customer is Gen Z and Millennial men aged sixteen to thirty five, a segment that drives sixty percent of global streetwear demand and spends three hundred to five hundred dollars a year on the category, mostly discovered through TikTok and Instagram. The beachhead is even younger, Gen Z aged sixteen to twenty four, who discover new fashion almost entirely through organic social content.
